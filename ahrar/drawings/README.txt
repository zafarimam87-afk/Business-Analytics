HOW TO ADD AHRAR'S DRAWINGS
============================

1. Save photos/scans of Ahrar's drawings into THIS folder (drawings/)
2. Name them exactly like this:
   - drawing1.jpg
   - drawing2.jpg
   - drawing3.jpg
   ... up to drawing20.jpg

3. Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .gif

4. Reload the website and they will appear in the "My Drawings" gallery!

TIP: You can take a photo of his paper drawings with a phone and save them here.
     No scanner needed!
